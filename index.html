I / A little about me

I began my Ph.D in Software Engineering (SE) at Monash University in 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Aldeida Aleti, Dr. Xiaoning Du, and Dr. Neelofar. Previously, I received both my Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees from the University of Engineering and Technology, Vietnam National University, under the supervision of A/Prof. Pham Ngoc Hung.

III / Research interests

Trustworthy AI and Testing

I am especially interested in testing the trustworthiness of AI-based tools for coding tasks, ensuring they provide explainable outputs aligning with SE standards.

LLMs for Code

I'm currently visiting Prof. David Lo's research group at SMU and working on TITAN, a collaborative SMU-GovTech project on intelligent vulnerability discovery.

Traditional Software Testing

From 2018 to 2024, I led a team in collaborative projects with FPT Software Ltd. and Viettel Group on automated unit testing, code change analysis, and regression testing.
